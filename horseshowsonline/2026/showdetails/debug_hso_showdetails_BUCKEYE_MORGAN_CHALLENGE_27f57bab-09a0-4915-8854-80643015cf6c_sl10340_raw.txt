Dressage Awards Presentations
Classic Dressage Introductory Level – Amateur Rider
Our CHAMPION is Joy Ollier riding Millenniums Masterpiece GCH owned by Danielle Burkett of Adrian MI. This pair also scored the Highest test score of the entire show by earning a 77.188%
Classic Dressage Training Level – Amateur
Our CHAMPION is Dr. Maureen Nelson riding Morgan Valley O’Malley GCH owned by Danielle Burkett of Adrian MI. This pair also earned Reserve Champion Amateur in Western Dressage Basic Level.
Western Dressage Introductory & Basic Level - Amateur
Our CHAMPION for both of these divisions is Catherine Mast riding RCV Stolen Moments owned by Scott Nevers of Ypsilanti MI. This pair also earned Reserve Champion Amateur in Classic Dressage Introductory Level amateur and Champion Sport Horse In Hand with Lilli Blair handling.
Western Dressage Introductory Level – Open
Our CHAMPION is Danielle Burkett riding AMD Here Comes Treble owned by Danielle Burkett of Adrian MI. This pair also earned Reserve Champion Open in Western Dressage Basic Level.
Classic Dressage Training Level – Amateur
Western Dressage Basic Level and Western Dressage Level 1 – Open

Western Dressage Introductory Level – Junior Exhibitor
