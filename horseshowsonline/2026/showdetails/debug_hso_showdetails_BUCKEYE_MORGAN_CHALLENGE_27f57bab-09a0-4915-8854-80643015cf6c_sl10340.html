Dressage Awards Presentations

Our CHAMPION is Joy Ollier riding Millenniums Masterpiece GCH owned by Danielle Burkett of Adrian MI. This pair also scored the Highest test score of the entire show by earning a 77.188%

Classic Dressage Training Level – Amateur

Our CHAMPION is Dr. Maureen Nelson riding Morgan Valley O’Malley GCH owned by Danielle Burkett of Adrian MI. This pair also earned Reserve Champion Amateur in Western Dressage Basic Level.
